One step to go

atelier needs two permissions, which Firefox asks for one at a time, each with a click:

  • Access to your sites, to apply your styles to them, and for the agents you ask for changes to see the pages they're working on (in tabs of their own; yours are only read for a screenshot when you ask).
  • User scripts: the only way to get a style into a page before it first appears, so you never see it unstyled. atelier only runs its own code, which inserts the styles from your atelier directory into the pages they're written for.

Ready

atelier is serving your styles from (theme: ).

To style a site, open it, click atelier's toolbar button, and choose Style this site. An agent session does the work: start one in your agent harness (in Claude Code, atelier's /mcp__atelier__work prompt), or have atelier start one for you with [session] in its config.toml.
